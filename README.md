# SmartFleet: SME Fleet and Cash Flow Assistant

Cloud based fleet management platform for South African transport SMEs.
Built by **The Guys in Tech** for the FNB App of the Year Hackathon 2026.
Challenge Area 6: Climate Resilience and Essential Services.

## The Problem

South Africa has over 250,000 transport operators, and most of them are running their fleets on spreadsheets, WhatsApp, and paper logbooks. They lose thousands every month to fuel theft, unexpected breakdowns, and costs they cannot see until month end.

## The Solution

SmartFleet is a cloud based fleet management platform that turns scattered operational data into realtime intelligence. It helps transport SMEs cut costs, prevent breakdowns, and reduce their environmental footprint.

## Core Modules

- **Vehicle Registry** Track every vehicle profile, status, and performance history
- **Trip Management** Log trips with distance, fuel used, and cost per journey
- **Fuel Tracking** Record fill ups, calculate consumption, and flag anomalies
- **Maintenance Scheduler** Predictive scheduling that alerts before breakdowns happen
- **Cost Analytics** Realtime dashboard showing spend by vehicle, driver, and category
- **AI Insights Engine** Forecast costs, detect anomalies, and recommend optimisations
